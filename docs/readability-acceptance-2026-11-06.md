## Research applied

- [Australian Government Style Manual: literacy and access](https://www.stylemanual.gov.au/accessible-and-inclusive-content/literacy-and-access): technical readers benefit from plain language too. Use familiar wording without losing necessary technical meaning.
- [UNECE: Making Data Meaningful, part 2](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/documents/64157/4374310/33-UNECE-making-data-meaningful-Part2-EN.pdf/d5b954e2-b110-469b-a2b5-78aa7c12ab62): sensible rounding helps comparison and recall; retain enough information for the intended use. Display policy is separate from underlying arithmetic.
- [Nielsen Norman Group: progressive disclosure](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/progressive-disclosure/): put the common task first and label secondary detail clearly; do not hide essential risks or create multiple competing paths to the same task.
- [Luxor: Hashprice definition](https://docs.luxor.tech/hashrateindex/hashprice): distinguishes expected mining revenue and its inputs from the additional costs needed for an operating decision. No competitor endorsement or invented live benchmark.

These principles guide the changes; their exact application is an editorial/design judgement. This is not a claim of user testing.
