## Fact preservation

- Hashrate remains the exposure and difficulty determines the outcome at the observation block. Observation and settlement blocks remain separate fields. Threshold equality and the Yes/No conditions are unchanged.
- Winning payout remains fixed per share, independent of the distance beyond the threshold. Profit or loss still depends on position cost and fees. No return, worked financial example or hedge coverage was invented.
- Collateral still comes from each selected market. No universal token, custody, legal, accounting or eligibility claim was added.
- Indicative prices remain in the selected currency per unit of winning payout, are neither forecasts nor executable quotes, and do not promise liquidity. Fees, depth, partial fills, exits, funding and operating risks remain named.
- Public reading is distinct from organisational approval. The private-link warning and the lack of a specified minimum fleet size or capital amount remain.
- All risk wording and existing app/control labels were preserved. The professional-investor restriction in the shared page shell was outside these files and unchanged.
- TypeScript AST comparison against HEAD confirmed matching node kinds/identifiers, all JSX attributes, numeric literals and URL/anchor strings. This supports preservation of JSX structure, props, IDs, actions, bindings and calculation logic. Manual diff review confirmed visible-copy-only changes.
