# V3 operating design packet — prototype only

Status, 22 October 2026: templates and proposed operating definitions. No target list has been populated, person contacted, service activated, subscriber created, forecast published, DOI deposited or trade executed through this packet. Empty fields mean **UNASSIGNED / UNAVAILABLE**, never zero results. The current newsletter and scenario-email experiences are local previews; the review log is controlled local QA, not a visitor collector.

Use alongside `v3-newsletter-prototype.md`, `research/PROTOTYPE_RESEARCH.md`, the `v3-measurement/` dictionary and observation/report templates, and `v3-canonical-migration.md`. These describe implementation and launch boundaries; this packet adds ownership, editorial and business operations. No external action is authorised by an empty template.

## 1. Assign the two tracks

| Track | Proposed responsibility | Named owner / deputy | Required evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Published reader track: discover → return → identify → qualify | Marketing/editorial, with source and publication reviewers | UNASSIGNED | Published item/version, source receipt, channel record, confirmed consent and permitted match evidence |
| Direct finance track: qualified organisation → conversation → first/repeat position | Founder/commercial owner, with an authorised finance/operations contact | UNASSIGNED | Approved target organisation, permitted introduction, actual discussion, approvers, next action and confirmed execution records |
| Data / model | Research owner and independent reviewer | UNASSIGNED | Reproduction, observation provenance, pre-outcome forecast receipt and correction history |
| Privacy / delivery / execution | Responsible service owners and appropriate reviewers | UNASSIGNED | Approved purposes, access/retention, end-to-end delivery tests, verified execution evidence |

Start both tracks when operations are authorised. Educational readers can use public findings and tools without becoming prospects. CFOs/treasurers, funds/desks and lender credit teams need their own exposure question, mandate, funding/custody constraints and approvers; job title or fleet size alone does not establish suitability. Use the institutions review and forwardable scenario brief; record who else must approve before proposing execution.

**Blank decision record:** organisation reference / volunteered role / business question / analyst or internal champion / approvers and unresolved questions / permitted contact route / next action / owner / due date / evidence links. Keep personal and confidential entries in an approved restricted system, never this repository or routine analytics.

## 2. Retarget editorial release and distribution

**One release record per epoch:** target height; observed adjustment and source capture; forecast versions and matched lead times; error/interval evaluation; what changed and why; limitations; correction notes; complete public finding URL; printable report/dataset version and digests; useful next calculator action; editor/reviewer; planned/actual publication time; delivery/receipt evidence.

Release checklist:

- Preserve the original source bytes and previous versions. Publish complete findings publicly; email is an optional delivery format, not a gate to findings.
- Separate observed historical changes, synthetic worked examples and prospective forecasts. The prototype's frozen observed dataset does not establish forecast performance.
- Capture each relevant public estimator and Doefin at agreed matched lead times each epoch. Log missing providers, unavailable intervals and methodology differences; never quietly select winners. Preserve issue-time source receipts before outcome and before any related market listing.
- Publish the evaluation, including error and interval width/coverage where supplied, after the outcome. Preserve misses, corrections and original records. Publish no aggregate ranking until timing and cohort comparability have been reviewed.
- Prepare a research issue: retarget result, changes, forecasting errors, method/data links and next observation. Keep product-update preference separate. Publication alerts require a real publication trigger and explicit opt-in.
- Have named editorial/source reviewers approve the release. Test requested-resource delivery and recurring consent/unsubscribe independently before any actual send; log publication, delivery, engagement and later return separately.

**Podcast / independent analyst method brief:** research question / reproducible method / frozen dataset version and source receipts / uncertainty and failure cases / what is still unknown / permitted citation / contact for methodological questions. Invite scrutiny, reproduction and disagreement; do not require a favourable conclusion. Any early data access must state release/embargo terms and must not imply independently verified findings. Record actual appearance or independent publication URL after it happens.

**Channel record:** item ID/version / intended audience / channel and approved source tag / owner / permission or community rule / actual placement URL/date / cost and verified rate-card date if any / named citations / return cohort evidence / next decision. Prefer relevant free newsletter recommendations and human useful answers over announcement posts. Check Reddit/community rules before participation. Validate X's relevant audience before allocating effort. Treat conferences, LinkedIn, mining press, podcasts and partner introductions as separate experiments; do not reuse old reach or cost estimates as current facts.

## 3. Named-organisation list and voluntary matching

Create an approved **50–100 organisation** working list in a restricted CRM/list after deciding its scope. Do not invent organisations to meet the count. Organisation-level schema:

```csv
org_id,organisation_name,website_domain,segment,fit_hypothesis,source_url,source_checked_at,target_list_owner,permitted_contact_basis,volunteered_match_status,match_evidence_ref,decision_maker_status,approvers_status,last_substantive_contact_at,next_action,next_action_owner,next_action_due_at,stage,evidence_refs,do_not_contact
```

The header is an empty template, not an actual prospect list. Optional training example, explicitly fictional: `DEMO-001 / Fictional Mining Co / fictional-miner.example / miner / DEMONSTRATION ONLY`; never import it into the active list or report it as a named target. Keep any contact person's identity in a separately permissioned record.

Match workflow:

1. Confirm the requested action and permission; a one-time report request is not newsletter consent. Preserve the four declared-role choices: miner, fund/desk, service provider, other. Record unknown instead of inferring a role.
2. Use company/domain information only when volunteered or otherwise specifically permitted. The current prototype does **not** infer a company domain from email. Record the evidence and uncertainty; ambiguous/shared domains require human review.
3. Compare with the approved list and the organisation's stated question. A calculator can offer optional scale/context, but do not capture raw confidential inputs in routine analytics. No universal fleet/capital cutoff is defined by this packet. Restrict any legitimately needed detailed analysis to the approved review system.
4. Assign a human to a valuable confirmed match within **one day**; proposed operational SLA is 24 hours from confirmed match. Record match time, owner, response due/actual time and any delay reason. Outbound action still requires permission and the applicable channel rules.
5. Follow up with the relevant method, scenario or due-diligence question, rather than a generic sequence. Record actual reply, approvers and next action. Honour do-not-contact, preference and deletion requests through the approved service process.

## 4. Partner, liquidity and first/repeat review

**Partner/introduction record:** organisation / channel type (mining finance, advisory, brokerage, energy or other) / trust evidence / named decision-maker introduction requested and actually accepted / substantive discussion evidence / owner / next action. Measure introductions reaching a decision-maker, not introductions merely offered.

**Liquidity readiness record:** contract and observation block / timestamped book snapshot / usable depth at agreed price bands and sizes / spreads / partial-fill and exit constraints / designated maker arrangements and obligations / funding and operational checks / responsible reviewer. Observe depth before, during and after any incentive; a transient quoted balance is not durable executable liquidity.

**Incentive proposal, inactive:** purpose / intended participant / referral or tier mechanics / budget / duration / abuse/conflict risks / disclosure and terms / responsible legal/commercial approval / liquidity dependency / stop conditions / post-incentive depth measurement. Do not activate rewards before review or substitute rewards for usable liquidity.

**Guided first-trade record:** participant's mandate and approvers / reviewed contract, collateral, fees and loss/settlement limits / authorised support owner / actual questions resolved / confirmed position/fill receipt / assistance given / unresolved operational issues. No website click proves a fill. After settlement, prepare a factual position/settlement report, verify authorised delivery, offer the next retarget's research, and observe actual repeat activity without promising returns.

## 5. Measurement contract and six rate templates

These definitions are **proposals to agree before collection**. Use `numerator / eligible denominator`, show both counts and unknowns, and mature each cohort for its full observation window. Do not turn local QA previews into subscriber or trade outcomes. Reliable, permitted cross-session identity and organisation matching do not yet exist. Exclude internal/bot/test traffic by an agreed policy; report unmatchable visitors separately rather than treating them as non-converters.

| Rate | Proposed numerator | Denominator and proposed window |
|---|---|---|
| F01 Awareness → return | First visitors with an independently initiated return in the later fortnight; exclude campaign-click returns from the **unprompted** numerator | First-visitor cohort with permitted reliable identity and 28 complete observation days; later fortnight means days 14–27 after first visit |
| F02 Return → identify | Returning readers completing confirmed identification: newsletter opt-in, verified alert, or an actual delivered/accepted scenario request | Returning-reader cohort with 28 complete days after first qualifying return; report each identification action separately |
| F03 Identify → qualify | Identified people with a documented permitted target-organisation match or a reviewer-confirmed role/context fit | Confirmed identified-person cohort with 28 complete days; retain person-level denominator and separately report distinct matched organisations; no invented size threshold |
| F04 Qualify → conversation | Qualified organisations reaching an actual discussion with the appropriate decision-maker | Deduplicated qualified-organisation cohort with 28 complete days; record who attended and discussion evidence in restricted CRM |
| F05 Conversation → first trade | Organisations/counterparties with a confirmed first executed position after the conversation | Deduplicated conversation cohort, observed for three complete target epochs after conversation; agree organisation/counterparty mapping and partial-fill rules before reporting |
| F06 First → repeat trade | Distinct counterparties with a second executed trading occasion within three epochs of their first | First-trade counterparty cohort with three complete epochs of follow-up; distinguish separate occasions from multiple fills of one order and report epoch/time boundaries |

Person, organisation and counterparty are different units. F03's distinct-organisation output becomes F04's cohort after an explicit deduplication bridge; never multiply the six percentages into an implied end-to-end conversion rate. For F05/F06, agree whether a qualifying executed fill is sufficient or a minimum/settlement condition is needed; publish the agreed policy and do not change it to improve results.

**Outcome register:** named external citations and URLs (awareness); independently initiated later-fortnight returns (return); confirmed subscribers declaring miner/fund/desk (identification); distinct target organisations in contact (qualification); actual decision-maker conversations; distinct counterparties trading twice within three epochs; independent citations/quotes/links per literature item. Also record denominator, timing, source, unknowns and exclusions. Small counts require named-organisation evidence in restricted records and explicit counts; protect identities in shared reports. No statistical or causal claim follows from a sparse funnel.

## 7. Publication and DOI review gates

**Publication review questions, unanswered:** Who is responsible for deciding the permitted audience and content boundary in each relevant jurisdiction? What may remain ungated commentary? Which neutral navigation or promotional elements are permissible on commentary surfaces? What eligibility wording and disclosures are required, where and in what form? Is there any retail acquisition flow to remove? What review is required for requested reports, newsletters, alerts, partner messages and incentives? What durable receipt proves forecasts precede both outcomes and related listings? Who approves a later non-empty promotion layer? Record the responsible counsel/reviewer, question, written answer, scope, date, expiry/review trigger and implementation evidence. This packet does not answer legal questions or certify approval.

**DOI deposit checklist, inactive:** select an approved repository/registrant; confirm data redistribution rights/licence; freeze the dataset/report and original sources; verify version, dates, authors, methodology, limitations and distribution digests; prepare title, description, keywords, creator identifiers where authorised, landing URL and citation format; decide dataset-version versus concept-record relationships; test public access and machine-readable metadata; obtain owner/reviewer approval; deposit only when authorised; record the **actual assigned** DOI and receipt; verify resolution and citation; preserve earlier versions and publish corrections with reasons. Current metadata/readiness is not a registered DOI. Never manufacture a DOI or overwrite v1 to imply a new capture.

## 8. Quarterly review and traceability

Open the quarterly review with named-organisation progress: new relevant organisations, substantive conversations, required approvers, confirmed first/repeat counterparties, surviving usable depth, partner introductions reaching decision-makers and substantive outbound replies. Then inspect literature citations, return and identification. Traffic/subscriber growth without downstream organisation evidence is a diagnostic signal, not success. Record known confounders, missing evidence, actual spend, owner and keep/change/stop decision. Targets remain unset until an agreed baseline; no retrospective activity is fabricated.
